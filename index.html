About PaaniLink

Many neighbourhoods get piped water only a few hours a week, so families depend on tankers filled at City Water Board hydrants. PaaniLink replaces phone bookings with one online system, so a tanker, a driver or hydrant water is never promised twice, subsidy reaches the right people, and every delivery can be traced from booking to confirmation.
